Dioramas & scenes

A scene built around a moment: one figure on a vignette base, or a full narrative piece with several figures and scratch-built elements.

Large-scale display terrain

Boards and terrain sets painted as a whole, from a single building to a complete table, built to be photographed and displayed.

Display figures

Single figures and centerpieces painted for the shelf, not the tabletop, finished on a plinth.

  1. InquiryTell me the subject, scale, references and when you'd like it.

  4. DeliveryFinal photography, then packed for transit or handed over in Denver.
Classes & coaching

Learn at the bench

Classes

Small-group painting and terrain classes for shops, clubs and events. Topics range from display basing and weathering to building a diorama from scratch.

Virtual coaching

One-on-one video sessions on your own piece. We go through color, contrast, composition and finish, with feedback you can use at your next sitting.

Events & workshops

Available for conventions, store events and private groups. Ask about dates and travel.

About

I'm Evan Lapka, a miniature artist working in Denver, Colorado.

I started ATXminis in Austin, and spent years as a studio artist for Imperial Terrain, TurboDork and CRAFT, painting, building terrain and designing dioramas for production and Kickstarter campaigns. Now I take on a small number of private commissions, focused on dioramas, large-scale display terrain and single figures.

Featured in Voyage Austin. More work on Instagram @atxminis.
