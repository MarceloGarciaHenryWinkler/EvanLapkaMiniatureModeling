About

I'm Evan Lapka, a miniature artist working in Denver, Colorado.

I started ATXminis in Austin, and spent years as a studio artist for Imperial Terrain, TurboDork and CRAFT, painting, building terrain and designing dioramas for production and Kickstarter campaigns. Now I take on a small number of private commissions, focused on display pieces, dioramas and single figures.

Featured in Voyage Austin. More work on Instagram @atxminis.
